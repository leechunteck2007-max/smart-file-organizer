# Code of Conduct

Be respectful, patient and constructive. Welcome different backgrounds and experience levels. Focus criticism on work, honor privacy and accept correction gracefully.

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, sexualized conduct, unwanted contact and publication of private information are unacceptable.

Maintainers may remove contributions, warn participants or restrict participation proportionate to conduct. Report concerns through an available private maintainer contact; otherwise request one without publicly disclosing identities or incident details. Handle reports discreetly and without retaliation.
